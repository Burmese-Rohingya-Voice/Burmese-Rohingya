## English

### About this repository

This repository is the home of **Habi Ba (Su Su Maa)**, a freelance Medical Interpreter, Translator and AI Voice, Data & Language Specialist. It brings together information about my interpreting, translation, voice recording and data annotation work for Burmese and Rohingya, two languages that are still under-represented in language technology.

It is meant for:

- **Healthcare, legal and social service organisations** looking for a remote interpreter (OPI and VRI).
- **Language service providers** that need Burmese, Rohingya or Bengali coverage.
- **AI and speech teams** that need consent-based voice recording, transcription and annotation for Burmese and Rohingya.
- **Community groups** working with Rohingya and Burmese communities.

### Profile

I am a freelance interpreter and translator with **4+ years** of experience delivering over-the-phone (OPI) and video remote (VRI) interpretation in medical, mental health, legal, social and business settings. I have supported organisations including **IOM, MSF France, Lingotec and Voiceline Language Service**. I am trained in medical interpreting, HIPAA and FWA compliance and cultural mediation, and I am known for accurate, impartial and confidential communication that preserves meaning, tone and register.
